\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Summary of Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item NPC AI has evolved from fixed scripts and state machines to
        modular behavior trees, planners and utility systems.
  \item Each step traded some designer control for more flexible and
        believable behavior.
  \item Perception and movement systems are mature and shared across most
        games.
  \item No technique is universally best; commercial games rely on hybrids
        tuned to their genre (Table~\ref{tab:comparison}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Outlook}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Machine learning and LLMs open new possibilities, especially for
        conversation and social behavior~\cite{park2023,gallotta2024}.
  \item Their adoption depends on solving consistency, cost and control
        problems.
  \item The most likely near-term future is hybrid: classical architectures
        provide structure, while learned models add variety and language.
  \item Open questions: How do players perceive generative NPCs over long
        play sessions? How can designers author and test them?
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations of This Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Many industry techniques are documented only in talks and
        practitioner books rather than peer-reviewed papers.
  \item The comparison is qualitative and does not include new
        experiments.
  \item The LLM field changes quickly; findings reflect the state of
        research at the time of writing.
\end{itemize}
